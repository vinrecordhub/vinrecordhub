Buy 10 Vehicle History Reports for $54.99

The 10-credit bundle brings the price of a single vehicle history report down to $5.50 — a fraction of what 10 separate premium-brand reports would cost. Here's exactly what you get.

The 10-pack at a glance

$54.99 buys 10 report credits. That works out to $5.50 per report — each one redeemable for a full vehicle history report from Carfax® or AutoCheck®, your choice, decided individually for each VIN you check. There's no subscription, no recurring charge, and no expiration pressure forcing you to burn through all 10 credits by a deadline. You buy once and redeem on your own timeline.

What 10 separate reports would actually cost

Buying 10 vehicle history reports one at a time from a premium provider adds up fast. At roughly $40–$45 per single report — the typical retail rate for a one-off Carfax® purchase — 10 individual reports run somewhere north of $400. Even at AutoCheck®'s lower single-report rate of around $25, 10 separate purchases still land around $250. The VinRecordHub 10-credit bundle at $54.99 undercuts both by a wide margin, whichever brand you'd otherwise be paying full price for.

Who actually needs 10 reports?

Ten credits is more than a one-car buyer needs, and that's fine — the bundle exists for a different kind of shopper. It fits people who are genuinely cross-shopping: checking a dozen listings before narrowing to a shortlist, comparing multiple trade-in offers, or buying more than one vehicle over a few months and wanting the per-report cost locked in ahead of time. If you already know you're getting exactly one car, a single $6.99 report or the smaller 3-credit bundle at $17.99 makes more sense — see our full guide to sizing your bundle to check which fits your situation.

How the credits work

Every credit is generic — it isn't pre-assigned to Carfax® or AutoCheck® at checkout. When you're ready to check a car, you enter the VIN and pick which report you want for that credit. That flexibility means you can run all 10 as Carfax reports, all 10 as AutoCheck, or split them however your shopping calls for — including spending 2 credits on a single high-stakes VIN to get both brands' data. Our Carfax and AutoCheck bundle guide walks through exactly how that dual-report redemption works.

What's in each of the 10 reports

Buying in bulk doesn't change what's inside a single report. Every credit you redeem — whether it's your first or your tenth — pulls that provider's full report: reported accidents and damage, title brands (salvage, rebuilt, flood, lemon), odometer readings to flag possible rollback, and ownership/usage history where the data is available. There's no "bundle-tier" lite version. The $54.99 price covers 10 full reports, not 10 partial ones, and each is emailed to you separately within about 60 seconds of redeeming the credit.

A realistic use case

Say you're actively shopping for a used SUV and have eight listings you're seriously considering, plus you want to double-check your own trade-in before listing it. That's nine reports — the 10-credit bundle covers all of it with one credit to spare, at a fraction of what running nine individual premium reports would cost. Compare that to buying reports one at a time: by the fourth or fifth report, you'd already have spent more than the entire 10-pack costs.

How the 10-pack compares to other bundle sizes

$54.99 for 10 credits sits in the middle of the VinRecordHub bundle lineup, and the per-report price scales down from there as you go bigger: a single credit is $6.99, three credits is $17.99 ($6.00 each), 10 credits is $54.99 ($5.50 each), 15 credits is $74.99 ($5.00 each), and 25 credits is $99.99 ($4.00 each). The 10-pack is the natural landing point for anyone whose shopping goes beyond "just this one car" but who isn't running a dealer lot — it's enough headroom to check every listing on your shortlist without committing to the larger 15- or 25-credit bundles built for flippers and small dealers. Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get for $54.99?

10 report credits on your VinRecordHub account. Each credit redeems for one full vehicle history report, and you choose Carfax or AutoCheck for each one individually when you redeem it.

Do the 10 credits expire?

Credits are tied to your account and redeemed on your own schedule — there's no forced weekly or monthly usage requirement.

Can I get both Carfax and AutoCheck reports on the same car with this bundle?

Yes. Spend 2 of your 10 credits on one VIN to get one report from each provider, leaving 8 credits for other vehicles.

Is 10 reports overkill for one purchase?

If you only need one report, buy a single credit for $6.99 instead. The 10-pack is built for shoppers checking multiple cars, comparing listings, or buying more than one vehicle over time.
